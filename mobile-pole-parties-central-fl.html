Mobile Pole Party FAQ

Do you bring the pole?

Yes. April brings a professional-grade stage pole and handles setup and breakdown.

Does it attach to the ceiling?

No. The mobile setup uses a stage pole, so there is no ceiling drilling.

Do guests need experience?

No. The party is beginner-friendly and can be tailored to the group.

Can this be at a hotel or event space?

Yes, as long as the hotel, venue, host, or property owner allows it and the space is safe for setup.

How do I book?

Send the date, location, group size, party type, and space type through the contact form or Instagram.
